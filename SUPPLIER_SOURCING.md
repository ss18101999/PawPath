# Supplier Sourcing Guide

The store should not depend on random long-shipping products. Use supplier speed, inventory reliability, and product quality as the first filter.

## Recommended First Supplier Path

Start with **Spocket** or **Zendrop**:

- Use Spocket if you want a more curated US/EU supplier feel and branded invoice options.
- Use Zendrop if you want stronger fulfillment automation and a future path to private labeling.

Also check **TopDawg** and **Doba** for pet-specific US-stocked products.

Use CJdropshipping or DSers mainly for product testing when US-stocked alternatives are not available.

## Supplier Selection Rules

Only import products that meet most of these rules:

- Ships to the US in 3-7 business days, or no more than 7-12 business days with clear disclosure.
- Has order tracking.
- Has recent supplier activity or verified order history.
- Has multiple clean product images.
- Has product dimensions and material information.
- Has a clear return/replacement process.
- Has low variant complexity.
- Is not fragile, heavy, electronic-heavy, ingestible, or size-dependent.

## Product Vetting Score

Score each candidate from 1-5.

**Shipping speed:**  
5 = US 2-5 days, 4 = US 3-7 days, 3 = 7-12 days, 2 = 12-20 days, 1 = unclear.

**Product fit:**  
5 = directly matches PawPath niche, 3 = loosely related, 1 = generic pet item.

**Margin:**  
5 = supports 3.5x+ markup, 4 = supports 3x, 3 = supports 2.5x, 1-2 = weak.

**Creative potential:**  
5 = obvious short video demo, 3 = decent demo, 1 = hard to show.

**Return risk:**  
5 = low risk, 3 = medium risk, 1 = high risk.

Only launch products with a total score of 20+ out of 25.

## Products To Avoid At Launch

- Dog food, treats, supplements, or anything ingestible.
- Bark collars or electronic training products.
- Dog clothing with complex sizing.
- Fragile ceramic bowls.
- Large beds or bulky crates unless supplier economics are excellent.
- Medical or anxiety-treatment products with compliance risk.

## Test Order Checklist

Before running paid ads:

- Order the hero product to a US address.
- Record actual processing time.
- Record actual delivery time.
- Inspect packaging and product quality.
- Take your own photos and short videos.
- Confirm the product matches Shopify listing claims.
- Test refund/replacement communication if something is wrong.

## Scaling Rule

Do not scale ad spend above a small test budget until your supplier has successfully fulfilled at least one real order and tracking has worked end-to-end.
