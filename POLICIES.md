## Shipping Policy

**Page title:** Shipping Policy

PawPath Supply currently ships to customers in the United States.

### Processing Time

Orders are usually processed within 1-3 business days. Processing time may be longer during holidays, supplier restocks, or high-volume sales periods.

### Delivery Time

Most US-stocked items arrive within 3-7 business days after processing. Some supplier-fulfilled items may take 7-12 business days depending on warehouse location and inventory availability.

Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Weather, carrier delays, holidays, and address issues can affect delivery time.

### Shipping Rates

We offer free US shipping on orders over $50. Orders below $50 may be charged standard shipping at checkout.

### Tracking

When your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. Tracking updates may take 24-48 hours to appear after the carrier receives the package.

### Incorrect Addresses

Customers are responsible for entering the correct shipping address at checkout. If you notice an address mistake, contact us immediately. We cannot guarantee changes after an order has entered fulfillment.

### Multiple Packages

Some orders may ship in separate packages if products are fulfilled by different warehouses.

## Refund Policy

**Page title:** Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your order. If something is not right, contact us at `support@yourdomain.com`.

### Returns

We accept returns of unused items in original condition within 30 days of delivery.

To start a return, email us with your order number and reason for return. If your return is accepted, we will send instructions for where to send your item.

Items sent back without first requesting a return may not be accepted.

### Non-Returnable Items

We cannot accept returns for:

- Used pet products.
- Items with pet hair, odors, bite marks, stains, or damage.
- Gift cards.
- Final sale items.
- Products returned outside the return window.

### Damaged Or Incorrect Items

Inspect your order when it arrives. Contact us within 7 days of delivery if the item is damaged, defective, or incorrect. Include your order number and clear photos of the product and packaging.

If approved, we will offer a replacement, store credit, or refund depending on the issue and supplier availability.

### Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you if the refund is approved. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to post the refund.

### Return Shipping

Original shipping costs are non-refundable. Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect.
