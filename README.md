# PawPath Supply Shopify Launch Kit

This workspace contains a ready-to-use launch package for a US-market dropshipping Shopify store.

## Store Concept

**Brand:** PawPath Supply  
**Niche:** Dog travel, enrichment, and on-the-go feeding accessories  
**Target market:** US dog owners aged 24-45 who travel, hike, commute, or run errands with their dogs  
**Positioning:** Practical, clean-looking dog gear that makes everyday outings easier and calmer.

## Why This Niche

Pet products are a strong US dropshipping category because they are emotional, visually demonstrable, lightweight, and frequently purchased. The best angle is not "generic pet store"; it is a tighter problem set: dogs eating too fast, messy car rides, hydration during walks, and portable enrichment.

## Files

- `NICHE_RESEARCH.md`: niche decision, product rationale, risks, and supplier direction.
- `SHOPIFY_SETUP.md`: exact Shopify admin setup steps.
- `STORE_COPY.md`: homepage, product page, collection, FAQ, and navigation copy.
- `PRODUCT_CATALOG.csv`: Shopify product import draft.
- `SUPPLIER_SOURCING.md`: supplier selection rules, search terms, and product vetting.
- `POLICIES.md`: policy drafts for refund, shipping, privacy, terms, and contact pages.
- `MARKETING_PLAN.md`: ads, emails, offers, and launch testing plan.
- `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`: operational checklist before going live.

## Recommended Shopify Stack

- **Theme:** Dawn, Refresh, or Sense from Shopify's free theme store.
- **Dropshipping app:** Start with Spocket or Zendrop for US shipping speed; use CJdropshipping/DSers only if you are comfortable with longer shipping or supplier testing.
- **Reviews:** Judge.me Product Reviews.
- **Email/SMS:** Shopify Email first; Klaviyo later when sales validate.
- **Analytics:** Shopify Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Analytics 4.
- **Trust:** Shopify Inbox, policy pages, tracking page, clear delivery times.

## Important Limits

I cannot log into your Shopify account, enter payment details, buy a domain, or make legal decisions for you. Use these files to set up the store inside Shopify, then authenticate Shopify CLI or share specific non-sensitive admin tasks if you want help deploying a theme later.
