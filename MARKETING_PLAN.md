# Marketing Plan

## Launch Offer

Use one simple offer at launch:

**Free US shipping on orders over $50.**

Optional launch discount:

**15% off first order with code `FIRSTPATH15`.**

Do not stack too many discounts at launch. The goal is to validate product demand, creative angles, and supplier reliability.

## Core Message

PawPath Supply helps dog owners make everyday outings easier with practical travel, feeding, car, and enrichment gear.

## Primary Ad Angles

### 1. Messy Car Problem

**Hook:** "POV: your dog had the best park day and your back seat paid the price."  
**Product:** RoadGuard Waterproof Dog Car Seat Cover  
**CTA:** Shop car essentials

Video structure:

1. Show muddy paws / fur on seat.
2. Show seat cover installed.
3. Show dog jumping in.
4. Wipe-clean demo.
5. Bundle CTA.

### 2. Dog Eats Too Fast

**Hook:** "If your dog finishes treats in 10 seconds, try this."  
**Product:** PawPath Portable Slow Feeder Lick Mat  
**CTA:** Shop slow-feeding gear

Video structure:

1. Show fast snack behavior.
2. Spread soft treat on mat.
3. Dog focuses on mat.
4. Show easy cleaning.
5. Mention supervised use.

Avoid medical claims. Do not say it prevents bloat, anxiety, or disease.

### 3. Walks Without Pocket Chaos

**Hook:** "Everything I bring for a 30-minute dog walk."  
**Product:** PocketPup Treat Pouch + TrailSip Water Bottle  
**CTA:** Build your walk kit

Video structure:

1. Empty pockets full of treats/bags/keys.
2. Load treat pouch.
3. Fill water bottle.
4. Walk scene.
5. Bundle CTA.

### 4. Dog Outing Kit

**Hook:** "The 3 things I keep in the car for every dog trip."  
**Product:** Road Trip Bundle  
**CTA:** Shop the road trip bundle

Video structure:

1. Car seat cover.
2. Seat belt tether.
3. Water bottle.
4. Dog in back seat.
5. Checkout offer.

## Organic Content Calendar

Post 2 short videos per day for the first 14 days.

Content mix:

- 40% product demos.
- 25% relatable dog-owner problems.
- 20% educational tips.
- 15% bundle/offers.

Example posts:

- "3 things I wish I kept in my car as a dog owner."
- "How to make a lick mat in 30 seconds."
- "What I pack for a dog park trip."
- "Before and after: muddy paws vs seat cover."
- "Dog walk essentials under $30."
- "How to keep your dog hydrated on summer walks."

## Paid Ad Testing

Start with TikTok or Meta, not both, unless you have enough budget.

Minimum test:

- 3 products: slow feeder, water bottle, car seat cover.
- 3 creatives per product.
- 2 hooks per creative.
- $20-$50 per day for 5-7 days.

Kill ads if:

- CTR is weak after meaningful impressions.
- Add-to-cart rate is very low.
- Comments show shipping or trust concerns.

Keep ads if:

- People watch past the first 3 seconds.
- Add-to-carts appear quickly.
- Comments ask where to buy or request variants.

## Launch KPIs

Track these numbers:

- Conversion rate.
- Add-to-cart rate.
- Average order value.
- Cost per add to cart.
- Cost per purchase.
- Gross margin after product cost and ad spend.
- Refund/chargeback rate.
- Supplier delivery time.

## First 30 Days

Week 1:

- Build store.
- Import products.
- Place test order.
- Post 10-15 organic videos.

Week 2:

- Launch small ad tests.
- Collect comments and objections.
- Improve product pages based on objections.

Week 3:

- Push bundles.
- Add real reviews if available.
- Test email discount vs free shipping.

Week 4:

- Cut weak products.
- Keep 1-2 winners.
- Order UGC content or create your own demo videos.
