# Shopify Setup Steps

Use this as the exact admin checklist for creating the store on Shopify.

## 1. Create The Shopify Store

1. Go to Shopify and create a new store.
2. Store name: `PawPath Supply`.
3. Business category: pet supplies / dog accessories.
4. Currency: USD.
5. Market: United States.
6. Set your store address and legal business details.

## 2. Choose Theme

Start with a free Shopify theme:

- **Dawn:** fastest and most flexible.
- **Refresh:** good for health/wellness style brands.
- **Sense:** warmer visual style for lifestyle products.

Recommended: use **Dawn** first because it is clean, fast, and easy to customize.

## 3. Brand Settings

Suggested brand identity:

- Brand name: `PawPath Supply`
- Tagline: `Easy outings for happy dogs.`
- Colors:
  - Forest: `#234236`
  - Sand: `#F4EBDD`
  - Clay: `#C87941`
  - Charcoal: `#1F2421`
  - Cream: `#FFF9F0`
- Fonts:
  - Heading: Shopify's default clean sans serif or "Montserrat" if available.
  - Body: Shopify's default system font or "Inter" if available.

Logo direction:

- A simple paw + path line icon.
- Avoid cartoon-heavy style.
- Use a clean lifestyle brand look.

## 4. Create Collections

Create these collections:

- `Travel Gear`
- `Feeding & Hydration`
- `Car Essentials`
- `Enrichment`
- `Bundles`

Use automated conditions based on product tags:

- Product tag equals `travel`
- Product tag equals `feeding`
- Product tag equals `car`
- Product tag equals `enrichment`
- Product tag equals `bundle`

## 5. Import Products

Use `PRODUCT_CATALOG.csv` as your product draft.

Steps:

1. Shopify admin -> Products -> Import.
2. Upload `PRODUCT_CATALOG.csv`.
3. Import as draft or active depending on your readiness.
4. Add supplier product images from your dropshipping app or upload final images manually.
5. Replace placeholder SKUs if your supplier app generates its own SKUs.
6. Confirm each product has the right price, variants, inventory behavior, and shipping profile.

Note: supplier apps like Spocket/Zendrop/CJ often import products directly. In that case, use the CSV as your copy/pricing guide instead of importing it first.

## 6. Install Supplier App

Recommended path:

1. Install **Spocket** or **Zendrop** from the Shopify App Store.
2. Search the supplier keywords from `NICHE_RESEARCH.md`.
3. Prioritize US-shipping products.
4. Import 6 products only at launch.
5. Edit all imported titles and descriptions to match `STORE_COPY.md` and `PRODUCT_CATALOG.csv`.
6. Run a test order for the hero product before scaling ads.

## 10. Store Settings

Payments:

- Enable Shopify Payments if available.
- Enable PayPal.
- Enable Shop Pay.

Shipping:

- Create US shipping zone.
- Offer free shipping over $50.
- Offer standard shipping below $50.
- Show delivery estimate: `3-7 business days for US-stocked items. Some products may take 7-12 business days depending on supplier availability.`

Taxes:

- Configure Shopify Tax for US sales.

Checkout:

- Require email and shipping address.
- Enable customer accounts if desired.
- Turn on abandoned checkout emails.

Notifications:

- Edit order confirmation and shipping emails to include your delivery estimate and support email.

## 11. Analytics

Install before launch:

- Meta Pixel.
- TikTok Pixel.
- Google Analytics 4.
- Google Search Console after domain connection.

## 12. Domain

Buy a domain only after checking brand availability. Preferred options:

- `pawpathsupply.com`
- `shoppawpath.com`
- `trypawpath.com`

## 13. Launch Rule

Do not launch paid ads until:

- Supplier delivery estimate is confirmed.
- At least one test order is placed.
- Product pages have final images.
- Policies are visible in footer.
- Checkout has been tested with a real payment method or Shopify test mode.
