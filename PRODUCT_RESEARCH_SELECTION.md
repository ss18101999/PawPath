## Supplier Research Summary

### Recommended Supplier Path

Start with **Doba** and **TopDawg** for supplier validation because both show strong US-oriented dropshipping positioning and Shopify-compatible workflows.

Use **Spocket** and **Zendrop** as secondary sourcing options if product quality, delivery time, or variant availability is better.

### Why These Suppliers

- Doba has US-stocked pet products and Shopify-oriented selections, including portable dog water bottles, car seat covers, and SodaPup enrichment/slow-feeder products.
- TopDawg emphasizes verified US suppliers, Shopify integration, real-time inventory sync, and faster US shipping.
- Spocket has pet travel products like portable water bottles and waterproof car seat covers.
- Zendrop has a broad pet catalog and direct Shopify integration for product import and fulfillment automation.

## Important Next Step

The products in Shopify are branded catalog placeholders with polished PawPath copy and pricing. They are not yet connected to supplier fulfillment.

Before accepting real customer orders:

1. Install the selected supplier app.
2. Match each PawPath product to a real supplier SKU.
3. Replace placeholder product records or connect variants to supplier-imported products.
4. Add supplier product images or original UGC images.
5. Confirm shipping costs and delivery estimates.
6. Place test orders for the hero product and car seat cover.
7. Remove the `supplier-needed` tag after fulfillment is confirmed.
