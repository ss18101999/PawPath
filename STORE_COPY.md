## About Page

We curate practical dog gear for real-life outings: daily walks, park days, errands, road trips, and weekends away. Our focus is simple: products that are easy to use, easy to clean, and actually useful.

From portable hydration to enrichment tools and car protection, every item in the shop is chosen to help you spend less time managing chaos and more time enjoying the path with your dog.

## FAQ Page

### Where do you ship?

We currently ship to customers in the United States.

### How long does shipping take?

Most US-stocked items arrive in 3-7 business days after processing. Some supplier-fulfilled items may take 7-12 business days. Delivery estimates are shown on product pages and in your order confirmation.

### Do you offer free shipping?

Yes. We offer free US shipping on orders over $50.

### Can I change or cancel my order?

If your order has not been processed by the supplier yet, contact us as soon as possible. Once an order has entered fulfillment, we may not be able to cancel or change it.

### Are these products safe for all dogs?

Always supervise your dog with feeding, enrichment, and travel products. Choose products that fit your dog's size, chewing behavior, and activity level. Discontinue use if an item becomes damaged.

### Do slow feeders prevent medical problems?

No product can guarantee prevention of medical issues. Slow feeders are designed to support slower eating and enrichment. If your dog has a health condition, ask your veterinarian for guidance.

### How do returns work?

Unused items in original condition can be returned within 30 days of delivery. See the Refund Policy for full details.
